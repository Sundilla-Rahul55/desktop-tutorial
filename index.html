Frequently asked questions

How do I book a reel-maker?

Tap Book now on any package. WhatsApp opens with your package name filled in. Send it, tell us your date and location, and we confirm your slot.

How fast will I get my reels?

Reels are edited on the spot and delivered the same day, so you can post while the moment is fresh.

What is included in a package?

Every package includes shooting time on site, a set number of edited reels and same-day delivery. Half Day and above also include music and captions.

How does payment work?

We share payment details on WhatsApp once your slot is confirmed. Prices are plus GST.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Message us on WhatsApp as early as you can and we will help you move your booking to a new date.

Is there a logo on my reel?

No. Your reels are yours, with no Rshoot logo on them.
