FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book a reel-maker?

Tap any Book now button. WhatsApp opens with your chosen package already filled in. Send us your date, time and location and we confirm your booking.

When will I get my reels?

The same day. Your reel-maker edits on the spot, so your reels are delivered ready to post while the moment is still fresh.

What is included in every package?

A trained reel-maker at your location, shooting on a pro-quality phone setup, on-the-spot editing and ready-to-post reels. Selected packages also include music and captions.

Will there be a watermark or logo on my reels?

No. Your reels are delivered clean, with no Rshoot logo, so they look like your own content.

How does payment work?

We confirm the price on WhatsApp before your shoot. You can pay by UPI or bank transfer. All prices are plus GST.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp as early as possible and we will help you reschedule or cancel according to our booking policy.
