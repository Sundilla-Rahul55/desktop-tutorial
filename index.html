Questions, answered

How do I book a reel maker?

Tap Book now on any package. WhatsApp opens with your package name filled in. Send it with your date and location and we confirm your slot.

When will I get my reels?

We edit on the spot. Quick Reel, Half Day and Full Wedding include same-day delivery.

What is included in a package?

Every package lists its hours and number of reels. Half Day adds music and captions.

How does payment work?

We share payment details once your slot is confirmed.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Message us as early as possible to change your booking.

Is there a logo on my reel?

No. Your reels are yours, with no Rshoot logo on them.
