FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book a reel maker?

Fill the booking form or tap Book now on any package and send your date and location. We confirm your slot right away.

When will I get my reels?

We edit on the spot. Most reels are delivered the same day, with the fastest in 2 hours.

What is included in a package?

Every reel package lists its hours of coverage and number of edited reels. Full Event and Wedding Day Reels also include raw footage.

What are the Wedding Premium Packages?

They bundle photography, stages, DJ, food, wedding cards and handmade collections into one planned celebration. Each total uses the lower end of the stage budgets, and we confirm your final quote on WhatsApp once we know your venue and date.

How does payment work?

We share payment details once your slot is confirmed. Reel package prices are plus GST.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Message us as early as possible and we will move your booking to a new date.

Is there a logo on my reel?

Yes. Every reel carries a small RSHOOT logo in the corner.
